On a first deployment, create the first Admin straight away: until then,
whoever opens /setup first becomes the Admin. Either:

  - open /setup at the address above, or
  - create the Admin from the pod, before anyone can reach /setup:

      kubectl exec -it -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} deploy/{{ include "score.fullname" . }} -- flask users create-admin

    Then sign in and set the public URL in Settings. /setup records it
    automatically; create-admin doesn't, and until it's set, reset and
    invite links are built from whatever host the request came in on.
